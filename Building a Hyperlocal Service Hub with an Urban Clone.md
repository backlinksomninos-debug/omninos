Establishing a rigorous verification process for professionals is the non-negotiable foundation of trust. This may involve background checks, license validation, or sample work assessments. Furthermore, designing a fair monetization model—be it a commission on bookings, a subscription fee for featured providers, or a hybrid—is essential for the platform's longevity and ability to reinvest in safety and features.
